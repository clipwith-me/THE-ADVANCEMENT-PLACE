# Product decisions (for client confirmation)

1. **Job board gate vs SEO.** *(Updated Nov 2026 — see 18.)* The client requires a complete profile before accessing the job board; the brief also asks for search-engine-visible job pages. Resolution: the searchable board (`/jobs`) requires a signed-in candidate with a complete profile; individual job adverts are public and indexable (applying still requires a complete profile). Admin › Settings › "Show job adverts publicly" turns adverts private too (they then leave the sitemap and are noindexed).
2. **Profile-completion policy.** Required: full name, email, phone, state, "Your experience" (a short summary; stored as `headline`), highest qualification (any level — SSCE, OND, HND, degree, etc.), at least one skill, a passport photograph (toggleable) and a CV (toggleable). NYSC status is optional and never blocks registration or applying.
3. **One application per vacancy**, enforced by the database. A withdrawn application can't be resubmitted for the same vacancy. Change on request.
4. **Statuses**: Submitted, Under review, Shortlisted, Interview, Successful, Not progressed (stored as UNSUCCESSFUL), Withdrawn (candidate only). Candidates see status history, not internal notes.
5. **Deadlines** are dates; applications close at the end of that day, Lagos time. Jobs past their deadline disappear from the board automatically.
6. **Employer names** are stored privately and shown only when the "authorised for publication" box is ticked; otherwise structured data names The Advancement Place.
7. **Email verification** is sent on registration but only enforced when `REQUIRE_EMAIL_VERIFICATION=true` (needs working SMTP).
8. **Freelance/outsourcing** is an enquiry service only — no bidding, contracts or payments, and the site says so.
9. **No fabricated content**: no statistics, testimonials, partner logos, staff photos, fees or employment guarantees. The trust section uses only verified facts (office address, hours, data handling).
10. **Header logo**: supplied mark + business name in text (lockup lettering is illegible at 40 px). Full supplied lockup is used on auth pages, About, and Open Graph.
11. **Passport photograph** (client request, Oct 2026) so staff can identify applicants. JPEG/PNG only, at least 150×150 px; GPS/device metadata (EXIF, XMP, comments, PNG text chunks) is stripped on upload, keeping only the orientation flag. Photos are private (owner + staff), are never shown publicly, travel with each application like the CV, and follow the same retention rule. Viewing a photo is not audit-logged (it appears in lists); CV downloads still are. Turn the requirement off in Admin › Settings.
12. **Address** follows the client's wording: Metroeyes Building, opposite Access Bank, Jide Jones Road, Oke-Ilewo, Abeokuta, Ogun State. The previous postal code was dropped because the client's address doesn't include one.
13. **Required fields on public forms** (client request, Oct 2026): phone number is required on the contact form (the team calls employers back); a CV is required for career assistance (to compare the candidate with the roles they want). Phone is also required on the freelance/outsourcing form; its attachment stays optional.
14. **Contact page** is pitched at employers ("Looking to recruit qualified and competent staff…"), and the homepage WhatsApp button pre-fills "I need assistance with applying for a job", both in the client's wording.
15. **Reviews** (client request, Oct 2026): anyone can submit a review (1–5 stars, name, email, "job seeker / employer / client", text) on `/reviews`. Every review starts as *Awaiting approval*; only reviews an admin publishes appear on the website (homepage and `/reviews`). Published reviews show first name + last initial; the email is never shown. No average score is displayed and no review structured data is emitted: an average of hand-picked reviews would mislead, and Google ignores self-published business reviews. Hidden reviews are deleted by the retention job after `RETENTION_DAYS`.
16. **About-page photos** are managed in Admin › Website photos (upload, caption, show/hide, reorder, delete). The gallery only appears once a photo is published — no stock or placeholder images. Uploads up to 15 MB are resized to 2000 px JPEG with metadata removed.
17. **Job flyers** are optional per job (JPEG/PNG resized to 2400 px, or PDF). A flyer is visible to signed-in users while its job is visible; like the full description, it isn't shown to signed-out visitors (see 18).
18. **Job pages need an account for the full details** (client request, Nov 2026). Signed-out visitors see a preview — title, category, type, arrangement, location, summary, posted date and deadline — and a "Sign up to see the full job" prompt; the description, responsibilities, requirements, salary, experience, skills, flyer and application instructions are only rendered for signed-in users (never sent to the browser otherwise). Admin › Settings › "Show job previews to visitors" (`publicJobPreview`) switches between this preview and a full sign-in wall. Trade-off: JobPosting structured data is no longer emitted for visitors, so jobs won't appear as rich results in Google's job search; the preview pages themselves can still be indexed.
